## 项目理念

这两份预设都希望让互动小说的回复更像自然接续的小说正文，而不只是对玩家输入作简短回应。

- **小说式接续：** 将玩家本轮已经给出的对白、动作与剧情事实自然融入正文，再继续描写角色反应。
- **人物表现：** 关注动作、眼神、神态、声音、停顿与身体语言，而不只依赖对白。
- **场景质感：** 让人物、空间、物件与环境共同参与叙事，形成更有层次的画面。
- **剧情连续性：** 尊重人物设定、世界书、聊天历史与已经发生的事实。
- **自然节奏：** 允许场景适度展开，但不强迫每轮长篇输出，也不替玩家擅自决定重要行动。

### 伊薇娅·织语

伊薇娅沿用 Tavo 默认预设的提示词槽位与组织方式，不额外加入独立叙事人格。设计目标是尽量减少额外身份引导与结构性改动，适合偏好中性、通用小说式描写的玩家。

### 伊芙琳·织境

伊芙琳采用自定义分层结构，具有独立的 Eveline 叙事身份，并保留针对成年角色亲密情感与行为的专门叙事指导。其目标是在模型与服务平台允许的范围内，让相关情节表达得更自然、具体，减少不必要的含糊或回避。

## 安装与使用

1. 选择适合自己叙事偏好的 JSON 文件。
2. 在 Tavo 中导入该文件。
3. 导入后检查提示词槽位、启用状态与顺序是否符合预期。
4. 建议先用熟悉的人物卡、世界书和模型进行体验，再决定是否调整规则。

**建议导入前备份当前预设。** 不同 Tavo 版本或导入方式可能存在差异，请以实际界面为准。

## English

### Overview

**Aozora-Tavo-Novel-Presets** provides two novel-style writing presets for Tavo. They share a narrative foundation but offer different prompt structures and experiences. They are not a basic-versus-premium pair.

### Choose a preset

| | **Iveya Zhiyu** | **Eveline Zhijing** |
|---|---|---|
| Focus | General novel-style writing | Novel-style writing with a distinct narrative identity |
| Prompt structure | Follows Tavo's default-style prompt-slot organization | Uses a custom layered prompt structure |
| Narrative identity | No additional standalone persona | Includes the Eveline narrative identity |
| Additional guidance | General prose, character portrayal, and continuity | Adds guidance for intimate emotions and behavior between adult characters |
| Best suited for | Players who prefer a relatively neutral, broadly applicable structure | Players who prefer a distinct narrative identity and more specific intimacy-oriented guidance |

- **Iveya Zhiyu 1.0.2:** [View / download JSON](presets/Iveya-Zhiyu-1.0.2.json)
- **Eveline Zhijing 1.0.2:** [View / download JSON](presets/Eveline-Zhijing-1.0.2.json)

Neither preset is inherently superior. More prompt modules do not guarantee better writing, and different models may respond differently to each structure.

### Shared design goals

- **Novel-style continuation:** Incorporate the player's latest in-story dialogue, actions, and established events into the prose before continuing with character reactions.
- **Character portrayal:** Use physical actions, gaze, expression, voice, pauses, and body language—not dialogue alone.
- **Scene texture:** Let characters, space, objects, and surroundings work together to create a more vivid scene.
- **Narrative continuity:** Respect character profiles, world information, chat history, and established facts.
- **Natural pacing:** Allow scenes to develop when appropriate without forcing every response to be long or making major decisions on the player's behalf.

### Installation

1. Choose the JSON preset that best matches your preferences.
2. Import it into Tavo.
3. Check the prompt slots, enabled states, and ordering after import.
4. Test it with familiar character cards, world information, and a model before making further changes.

Back up your current preset before importing. Behavior may vary across Tavo versions and import methods, so verify the result in your own setup.

### Compatibility and limitations

- Results depend on the model, API provider, character cards, world information, chat history, and generation settings.
- The project does not guarantee identical style, length, point of view, or content across models.
- Iveya is designed for broad applicability, but universal compatibility with all models and proxy services has not been established.
- Eveline's adult-intimacy guidance does not override a model's or service provider's restrictions and cannot guarantee specific output. Follow the rules of the model and service you use.
- This is an independent project, not an official Tavo project or an endorsement by Tavo or any model provider.
